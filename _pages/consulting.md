I have worked as an independent statistical advisor since 2013, alongside my role at Baylor College of Medicine. Clients have included Renovion, Baylor College of Medicine, UNC-Chapel Hill, and PGENI.

## What I can help with

- **Study design.** Protocols, statistical analysis plans, sample-size and power calculations, IRB forms, and clinical research instruments.
- **Analysis.** Interim statistics and exploratory outcome associations for pharmacological and medical-device studies; biomarker discovery in high-dimensional genomic data.
- **Communicating results.** Clinical data visualization and analysis for investor and regulatory audiences. One engagement contributed the analysis and preliminary clinical data visualization behind an orphan drug designation for an early-stage pharmaceutical company.
